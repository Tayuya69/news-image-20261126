A newspaper front page summing up the world news of Thursday 26 November 2026
in one image: the Houthis' claimed strike on Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery,
the Cairo Ring Road tanker fire, the US tariff proposals on 14 trading partners,
and ships reported hit by mines in the Strait of Hormuz.

- `entry.png` — the 1024×1024 front page, drawn from scratch with Python/PIL
  (`draw_entry.py`); no external images.
- `main.ts` — copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`
  (`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` stands in for `/output` outside the grader).
